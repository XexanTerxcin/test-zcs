Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Last Updated: May 7, 2026

ZUMS Computer Society ("ZCS," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy and security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, register for membership, sign up for events, or interact with our services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Identifiers: Full name, student ID, email address, phone number, department, and year of study.
  • Account Information: Username, password (stored in encrypted form), and profile preferences.
  • Event Data: Registrations, attendance records, team affiliations, and submitted files (CVs, project portfolios).
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device information, and usage logs collected via cookies and analytics tools.
  • Communications: Messages, feedback, and inquiries submitted through contact forms or email.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To manage membership accounts and verify student status.
  • To process event registrations, send confirmations, and coordinate logistics.
  • To communicate updates, newsletters, and announcements related to ZCS activities.
  • To improve our website, tailor content, and analyze user engagement.
  • To ensure compliance with university policies and applicable regulations.
  • To protect against unauthorized access, fraud, or security threats.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to enhance your browsing experience. These may include:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for core functionality such as authentication and form submissions.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our website.
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings and preferences for future visits.

You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information in the following limited circumstances:

  • University Administration: When required for official records, funding verification, or policy compliance.
  • Event Partners: With sponsors or collaborators solely for event coordination purposes, and only with your explicit consent.
  • Service Providers: With trusted vendors who assist in website hosting, email delivery, or analytics, bound by confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Obligations: When required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of ZCS members and the university community.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information, including:

  • SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmitted between your browser and our servers.
  • Hashed and salted password storage.
  • Regular security audits and access control restrictions.
  • Limited access to personal data by authorized ZCS executives and faculty advisors only.

While we strive to protect your data, no transmission method over the internet is 100% secure. We encourage members to use strong passwords and report any suspected security issues immediately.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, or as required by university record-keeping policies. Upon graduation or membership termination, non-essential personal data may be anonymized or securely deleted after a reasonable grace period.

7. Your Rights

As a member or website user, you have the right to:

  • Access, update, or correct your personal information through your account profile.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data, subject to university retention requirements.
  • Opt out of non-essential communications by adjusting notification preferences or contacting us directly.
  • Withdraw consent for specific data processing activities where consent was the legal basis.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for university students and faculty. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 16. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, please contact us for immediate deletion.

9. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external sites (e.g., social media platforms, partner organizations). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these third-party websites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. Significant changes will be communicated via email or website announcements. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us:
